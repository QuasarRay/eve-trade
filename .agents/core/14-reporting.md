# Reporting and Handoffs

During work, report only decisions, material discoveries, failures and next actions; do not narrate
every tool call.

Subagent handoffs are compact: conclusion, evidence locations, files inspected/changed, commands,
uncertainties, falsifiers, recommended next action.

Final reports separate: result; root cause/design; important changed files; executed validation and
outcomes; laws/acceptance gates; unresolved issues; workspace integrity. Never turn "not run" into
"expected to pass" or a child claim into observed proof.
